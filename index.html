lorre

a culture diary for your iPhone. log films, shows, books, and albums as stitches, and lorre writes them into a scrapbook zine.

need help?

open an issue on github and say what happened. include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can. no account needed to read answers here.

quick answers

  • where is my diary stored? only on your iPhone. there is no lorre account.
  • how do i back up my stitches? settings → export. it saves a JSON file you can keep anywhere.
  • how do i erase everything? settings → delete all data. this cannot be undone.
  • my lorre failed to write. your stitches are safe. try again in a bit, lorre keeps every stitch that is not yet in an issue.
  • a search did not find my film, book, or album. tap "no results, add it by hand."
